LRFD Truck Check App

Add this shortcut to your phone's home screen for one-tap access to the truck check form.

On iPhone / iPad (Safari):
  1. Tap the Share icon (square with upward arrow) at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top-right corner.
On Android (Chrome):
  1. Tap the Menu icon (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
